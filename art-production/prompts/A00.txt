Art direction: an original Japanese fairy-fantasy third-person 3D RPG, designed as a walkable illustrated world. Use the spatial openness, simplified sculptural forms and gentle light associated with Sky: Children of the Light as a reference for space and illumination; use GRIS as a reference for restrained watercolor tonal variation, limited color families, selective delicate linework and quiet composition. Invent the actual characters, symbols and architecture for this project.
Visual grammar: smooth readable silhouettes, broad matte color masses, selective detail, warm ivory light, cool blue-violet shadow, airy distance, and a shared motif of three petals cradling a seed. Main palette: ivory #F2EBDD, sage #9DAF9A, mist blue #A8C0CF, shadow blue-violet #747E9D, restrained gold #E8C879, small dusty-pink accents #CBA9B4. Palette values are appearance targets, not exact pixel constraints.
Avoid photographic surface noise, glossy plastic, dense realistic bark or pores, thick black cartoon outlines, omnipresent triangular facets, ornate unrelated symbols, neon saturation, text, logos, labels, watermarks and decorative borders. Use enough form and tonal separation to read at gameplay distance.
The specific deliverable below controls composition and camera. When references are supplied, use each only for the role identified in REFERENCE ROUTING; keep the project identity coherent without inheriting an irrelevant scene or pose.

DELIVERABLE:
Produce one target in-game frame, in a horizontal 16:9 composition. Third-person camera looking forward from behind a small traveler occupying about one sixth of image height near the lower center. A readable pale earth path curves through a broad sage meadow toward one tall ivory stone arch. Put one elegant clustered-canopy tree beside the arch, layered misty mountains in the distance, and a spacious luminous sky with a few large cloud masses. A tiny petal-shaped fairy hovers near the traveler. Keep the traveler a simple scale figure at this stage; detailed character identity will be fixed in separate references. Show plausible walkable ground, readable volumes and a calm focal hierarchy. Capture an achievable real-time 3D game view with painterly surface restraint, not a flat poster or an isometric diorama. The main read is light, space and silhouette; watercolor variation remains subtle.
